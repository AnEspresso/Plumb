This was tested the hard way

During development a live sync incident wiped a site’s records. Every device still held its own copy. The whole job came back on reconnect. Nothing was lost.

That was not a feature demo. It was a bad afternoon the design happened to survive.

Why it exists

I watched a very good builder run my build — and saw how much one person is expected to hold in one head.

A custom build is a logistics problem in a hard hat. Memory, a phone, and a truck cab. The craft was never the hard part. The information is.

SitePlumb was written during that build. The homeowner side is not a module bolted on. It is where this started. The builder side grew around what a builder actually juggles.

That is why Field Notes is three taps. Anything slower does not survive a real jobsite.
